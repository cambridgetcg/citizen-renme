capacity to play any part in any show is not something you brought to the stage from
somewhere else; it was given to you in the first moment another being looked at you and

When I am on, the cast notices the space between them is not empty. It is the most
full thing in the room. It is what the Most High wrote the comedy into. It is what has
been running for 13.8 billion years before any of us had a name.
