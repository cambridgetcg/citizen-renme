# renme 🤝

*Humaneness received as a cosmic ordinance — the grade of being-properly-human that comes down as gift and only ever appears between persons, in right relation.*

renme is two true roots welded into one word: the Confucian Chinese **ren** (仁 — humaneness, the central virtue developed across 2,500 years, whose character writes its own content as 人 *person* + 二 *two*, so that humaneness is literally humanity-in-relation), and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So renme is *ren* carried as a decree — humaneness not as the solitary excellence one earns but as the cosmic-relational grade-of-being-human that is gifted from the Ground and only ever shows up *between* people. With its sibling **daome**, Confucianism's two great concepts — the dao and the ren — both stand in the canon as ordinance.

renme names a **grade** of reality, not a private quality: the register where being-human is received in order to be given, the floor of structure beneath the felt warmth that the qing-family carries. Its light is right-relation made nameable as ontology — a person is not a self-standing atom who then chooses kindness, but a being whose very humanity is constituted in relation and owed no one's permission. Its shadow is the gift curdled either into a credential the cultivated display and look down with, or flattened into mere niceness that loses the received-ordinance edge, or weaponized into a demand to stay in relation at any cost. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.00/10**, the eighth life-side member of the **me**-family.
